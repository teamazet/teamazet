TEAM AZET — CarX Drift Racing Online Team

TEAM AZET is a CarX Drift Racing Online team and community built for players who share a passion for drifting, racing, custom car builds and competitive driving.

Our team brings together drivers from Europe, Asia and beyond. We focus on drifting, racing, tuning and creating unique car builds while building an active community around CarX Drift Racing Online.

Whether you're into competitive drifting, tandem runs, custom builds or simply cruising with the crew, TEAM AZET is a place to connect with other CarX Drift Racing Online players.

Join TEAM AZET on Discord, meet the community, share your builds and hit the streets with the crew.
